Quota Count (QC) values here are inferred per aircraft type, set so the mix matches the airport's Annual Monitoring Report 2025. The airport scores each airframe from its own noise certificate, and does not count dispensed movements, so its total will differ. Bristol's limits are 1,260 points for the summer season and 900 for the winter. Aircraft under 11.6 tonnes, helicopters and light aircraft are not scored. The weighted share uses the standard Lden penalties: an evening movement (19:00–23:00) counts about 3 times and a night movement (23:00–07:00) 10 times. Use these as a comparison between periods, not as a measured noise level.

How periods are decided. Each flight's clock time is matched against the airport's published night and shoulder windows. Wrap-around windows like 23:30–06:00 are handled correctly. A flight exactly on a boundary counts in the period that's starting.

Calendar date or night. A night runs across two dates. "Counting by night" moves movements after midnight back to the evening the night began, so each row is one night as residents experience it. Annual caps are counted by calendar year.

About the data. Flightradar24 movement lists are a best-available public record, not the airport's official log — a few movements can be missing or mislabelled. Treat the counts as well-evidenced indicators, not certified figures. Times are whatever Flightradar24 showed when you copied them: check its time setting is airport local time, not UTC, or summer movements will sit an hour out. The airport's own count against a cap may leave out emergency, exempt or dispensed movements, so it can be lower than the figure here. Noise scoring is indicative only.
